# Prospective resource feasibility before numerical qualification

Effective only after D108 publication and local commit, before any new native qualification. The user requires an unrounded **at least 1% complete-solve gain with no material tradeoffs**. All exactness, fixture, frozen sampling, fresh16, broader correctness, material-cost, holdout and fresh-original acceptance gates stay unchanged.

Compiler register allocations, per-block shared memory and theoretical occupancy are disclosed risk indicators. They are not alone measured speed, whole-device memory consumption or supported-input capacity. NVIDIA states that higher occupancy does not always improve performance in its [CUDA occupancy guidance](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cuda-c-best-practices-guide/index.html#occupancy). D108 therefore replaces the D094/D101 preliminary prohibition on increased allocated registers/static shared memory or reduced theoretical occupancy with a feasibility check. The previous helper remains intact as a diagnostic, and all differences remain visible.

Before numerical execution require: identical supported launch shapes and host/global payload/capacity; no new local/spill bytes; no increase in dynamic shared reservations; no decrease in maximum threads; positive driver occupancy at the fixed64/128/256-thread controls; default device shared/thread limits fit for every registered shape and the conservative1326-hand bound. ABI/caller contracts, intended emitted mechanism and unchanged entry bodies must pass independently. No launch limit, ABI defect, absent intended mechanism, quality change, failed numerical check or actual capacity loss is waived. Unmeasured contexts/devices remain unqualified. A candidate that advances still needs measured CPU/GPU memory, process CPU/responsiveness and compatibility checks before adoption. Faster primary timing alone cannot establish no material tradeoff.

For historical reuse, this supplement replaces D094/D101's future-source prohibition only for exact forms whose original application-kernel launches, GPU floating-result controls, solver runs and performance samples are all zero. CPU algebra/layout proofs are allowed. Inventory every canonical candidate through E065 and indexed resource record before selecting; disclose aliases and process the eligible forms in original canonical-ID order. Transfer only the original guards, widths, maps, arithmetic order and scope onto the accepted base. No tuning, combination, extra samples or retry after a new numerical or timing failure. Known failed numerical/timed forms and aliases stay closed. Frozen old protocols, labels, reports and outcomes remain unchanged; a fresh qualification is attributed to its original form, not called a new mechanism or reversal of a measured result.
