# Prospective qualification of previously unmeasured source forms

Effective after D101 publication, before any new candidate execution. The user's rule remains **at least 1% complete-cycle improvement with no material tradeoffs**. The allocation helper, accuracy requirements, workloads, stopping/storage settings, fixture guards, fresh16 confirmation and broader/material/holdout/fresh-original gates are unchanged.

D094 established that reported counts of 38 and 40 registers use the same rounded allocation on the verified compute12.0 target. That filter has since applied to new candidates. D101 audits the recorded resource-only rejections consistently under that same physical allocation test. Two source forms have no numerical or speed observations and failed solely on nominal counts; testing them cannot extend or select among their nonexistent timing samples.

This supplement expressly replaces D094's blanket prohibition on future reuse of closed source forms **only for the following class**. Original protocols, decisions, samples and labels remain frozen under their original rules. A future qualification is a separate, clearly attributed event; it must not be described as a new independent mechanism or as a reversal of an old performance result.

Eligibility requires all of these conditions:

- The original GPU work stopped at compilation/resource queries with zero application-kernel launches, GPU floating-result controls, solver runs and performance measurements. Prior CPU address/layout proofs may be retained. No failed floating-result/quality or timed hypothesis is eligible, including aliases with known outcomes.
- Every original failed gate was a nominal-register-count check. No spill, shared/global allocation, occupancy, capacity, ABI, mechanism or compatibility failure may be waived.
- All recorded resource pairs pass the unchanged D094 helper/proof. Before any numerical or timing work, revalidate the exact source form against the current accepted source and current device/runtime, with the fixed ABI, unchanged-entry, shared/spill/allocated-register and occupancy gates.
- Preserve the exact original mechanism, guards, widths, mapping, operation ordering and scope. Only mechanically carry it onto the accepted base. No source variant or combination with another rejected mechanism is permitted.
- Audit the whole indexed resource-only rejection set and process eligible forms in original ID order. Freeze source ancestry and eligibility before any fresh outcome. Every timing cohort is fresh; prior timings are never scored or pooled.
- Acceptance still requires all existing correctness, complete-cycle, per-fixture, fresh16, broader, material, holdout and cumulative gates. A resource result alone never qualifies a change.

The D101 inventory covers the canonical candidate registry and indexed compiler/resource reports. E031 retains shared-memory/occupancy costs; E043 retains allocation/occupancy costs; E046 and E059 cross an allocation quantum. E058's absent compiled mechanism and E060's measured river regression remain excluded. The eligible unmeasured forms are E050's fixed four-byte asynchronous staging and E057's fixed prefix-index mapping, in that order. Their historical rejections remain visible. Each requires new source-matched resource and complete numerical qualification before a fresh solve-speed experiment.

The eligibility review uses recorded compiler attributes and a calibrated NVIDIA SDK allocation model, not hardware allocation counters or proof of runtime speed. Its scope is the verified target. Any other device needs its own calibrated evidence. Root review is not an external independent audit.
