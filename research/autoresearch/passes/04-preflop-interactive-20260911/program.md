# Four-hour interactive preflop autoresearch

Window: 2026-09-10 23:03:27 UTC through 2026-09-11 03:03:27 UTC.

The user's requirement is a usable interactive Preflop Lab, approximately an order of magnitude faster than the already optimized coupled-deck implementation. Optimize time to a useful navigable strategy and spot export, not merely a faster iteration. The experiment may change the approximation if its quality and limitations are measured and communicated explicitly.

## Loop

1. State a bounded hypothesis and freeze its workload, measurements and quality gates.
2. Implement in the isolated worktree, preserving the production reference and live user session.
3. Measure fixed work, first usable strategy, and quality separately. Record failures and timeouts.
4. Retain only candidates supported by independent checks. Refine promising candidates within the window.
5. Validate and publish the resulting implementation and evidence. Deploy only if ready and the user's live work can be preserved.

## Research paths

- Compress the coherent latent showdown model into a cheap evaluator. Test representative-particle selection against naive subsets, including held-out range contexts. Keep pot conservation and explicit versioning. Do not revive the rejected normalized heads-up product.
- Publish a consistent early strategy without waiting for a full accuracy checkpoint. Separate preview iteration, measured accuracy and completion state; do not present uniform unlearned ranges as meaningful results.
- Evaluate policy decision loss under the full reference, and physical-deal equity error where independent evidence exists. A trained approximation of the coupled model inherits its limitations; it is not physical ground truth.

## Safety and measurements

Only one hardware benchmark at a time. Private server instances use distinct ports and owned process handles. Port 56708 is read-only during experimentation. Stop owned research work if a live solve or report starts. Retain source/binary/input hashes, native metadata, timing intervals, held-out splits and rejected outcomes.

Primary baseline is production ff54279, including last night's performance improvements. Do not compare against the slower pre-optimization version to inflate gains. Measure useful first strategy, ready-to-export spot, full trajectory, quality and allocation independently. Targets are experimental goals, not promises.

Acceptance requires correct save/load model identity, finite normalized policies, legal actions, stop/resume consistency, fixed/modelled-seat behavior, truthful preview labels and measured quality. An approximation is not required to be bitwise equal to the reference, but all reference-mode behavior must remain protected.
