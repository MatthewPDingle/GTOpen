What counts as an improvement?

Same game, actions, precision, 1,024 particles and solver updates. At least three paired runs, a repeatable gain of 3% or more on the large fixture, no material regression on the other fixture, and the required regression tests. A faster kernel alone is not enough.

The broader convergence problem is still open: previous methods could look accurate overall while leaving weak decisions in rare branches. This pass measures execution speed without changing those decisions.
