# Stronger lossless compression before fresh evaluation

The completed four-bank, 64-deal control and its independent review passed.
Its eight CPU/GPU query comparisons differed by at most 6.27e-13; the four
root checks differed by at most 1.20e-18. Independent replay checked 29,062
observations with maximum statistical difference 2.85e-14.

The subsequent study attempt stopped in preflight before registration, store
creation, or fresh sampling. Its unchanged conservative storage formula
projected 12,574,514,432 bytes, exceeding the 12 GB admission ceiling. The
original control, failed study log, and stopped pipeline remain preserved.

Fixed setup files were not the main cause. Most output bytes are archived
action probabilities. A new archive writer changes gzip level 1 to level 6;
the gzip format, original bytes, hashes, ownership rules, safe scratch release,
and original decoder remain unchanged. Four tests verify this restricted
change, byte-exact readback, repeated safe release, and retention of scratch
when the archive is corrupt or an unexpected file appears.

`complete-evaluation-gzip6-control-v1` then rearchived both completed control
batches into a new directory. All 14 decoded artifacts were byte-identical to
the original. The independent semantic batch reader rechecked all 64 reused
deals and 29,062 observations. No neural inference, GPU run, or fresh deal was
performed. Existing archives were not changed or deleted.

The new control occupies 6,493,797 bytes instead of 8,173,512. The original
whole-control extrapolation, including its full 50% margin and extra 20 MB,
now projects 9,994,472,192 bytes. The global 800 GB admission gate, 2 GB metadata
reserve, 40 GB destination free-space floor, and actual output cap remain.
Fresh global allocation is measured again before admission; this projection
alone is not permission to exceed the budget.

Its complete worker is structurally identical to the recovered study worker;
only the archive implementation and registered output prefix differ. The
independent reader differs only in output prefix. The four model banks,
