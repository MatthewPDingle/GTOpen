# Matched later-action training study

This is the proposed next scientific comparison after the implementation and independent-readback gates. It does not authorize a production deployment or imply that the new targets improve ranges. Before launch, register the exact sources, configurations, seeds, artifacts, stopping rules, and a fresh storage admission. The existing 800 GB combined allocated-file ceiling and 2 GB reserve still apply.

## Question and controlled change

Does replacing future-action sampling in positive-tag postflop training targets produce more reproducible and more effective preflop strategies, at the same number of sampled deals and network updates?

Use the same BB-versus-BTN-open context as the two completed action-integrated-root trials. Each candidate trial matches its corresponding old trial's original sampler, action, reservoir, and fitting seeds. Keep 78 updates, 512 physical deals per update, full original reservoir capacity, network architecture, feature definitions, 512 fitting steps, learning rate, and current-policy/averaging semantics. The only intended mathematical change is postflop target integration. The qualified captured-gradient fitter reduces implementation overhead; it does not change the objective. Preserve exact initial all-in estimators and the existing integrated BB root accumulator.

Run both fixed seed sets. No choosing a seed, checkpoint, number of updates, or averaging schedule based on prettier ranges. Evaluate the complete linear-weighted played bank, generations 0 through 77, excluding the unplayed final model 78. Existing implementation fixtures are not new held-out poker evidence.

## Training gates and storage

Require the two-update control and independent state/target readback before starting the full trials. Register each run separately, with complete-generation checkpoints and interruption recovery. A second run needs its own fresh capacity check. Measure the pilot's actual allocated bytes and project both trials plus an evaluation reserve before choosing a bounded lossless evidence format; do not assume the earlier trial's storage footprint still applies after adding traces.

The long runs must preserve reproducibility without recreating the earlier disk-pressure problem. Stop at resource or production-activity guard failures, preserving the completed checkpoint and partial-attempt evidence. Never resume a partial fit or partially applied generation. Any material implementation change after registration needs a new version and controls.

## Stability and accuracy are separate outcomes

1. **Stability:** report incoming-mass-weighted total variation between the two final candidate BB root policies, alongside the two baseline policies' value. Report all 169 classes and aggregate action frequencies. This is a two-seed reproducibility diagnostic, not an accuracy confidence interval.
2. **Effectiveness:** after both candidate histories are frozen and audited, evaluate baseline and candidate complete policies on the same unseen physical deals. Within each matched seed pair, cross old/new BB with old/new BTN. There are four profiles per seed, eight total. This tests changing one player's entire root-and-later strategy, addressing the limitation of forcing a new root action while leaving a rarely trained continuation in place.
3. **Interpretation:** report whether the new BB policy gains against each fixed BTN policy, and whether the new BTN policy gains against each fixed BB policy. Neither self-play value nor one head-to-head win alone proves approximate equilibrium quality. Improvements confined to one opponent, contradictory seed results, or unresolved confidence intervals are not grounds for promotion.

Provisional unseen evaluation budget: 65,536 population-sampled physical deals, sampling seed 382921, shared by all eight frozen profiles. No test samples are to be generated or inspected during implementation controls. Confirm resource feasibility and freeze this specification in an evaluation registration before dealing. Integrate future actions; retain sampled private cards and postflop runouts, and the existing conditional preflop all-in estimator. No future-card inputs enter policy inference.

Prespecify eight paired contrasts: for each of two training seeds, the new-vs-old BB gain against old BTN and against new BTN, plus the new-vs-old BTN gain against old BB and against new BB. Use one final look and simultaneous two-sided 95% family coverage via a stated Bonferroni construction. Report paired standard errors, effect sizes in bb per entry, and the assumptions behind the intervals. Do not silently extend the sample count after seeing an inconvenient result. The evaluation implementation and independent reader must verify common deals, mixed-profile transport, and payoff identities before that final study.

Root-only forced-action residuals may remain useful descriptive diagnostics, but cannot certify full best-response quality, particularly where a forced action inherits an early uniform continuation. They are not the primary effectiveness endpoint here.

## Scope still outstanding

This single heads-up, fixed-stack research context is an intermediate test. Even favorable results would not establish flexible preflop accuracy across positions, opening sizes, stack depths, rake structures, or multiway pots. Those remain part of the larger GTOpen objective and require subsequent transfer and coverage tests.
