# Consumer for the registered matched stack comparison

This implements the evaluation already fixed in
`STACK-CONTEXT-MATCHED-LEARNING-PLAN.md`. It is prepared while training runs;
preparation does not admit fresh testing or change the scientific design.

Require successful completion of all four 64-cycle training arms, actual release
of the training owner, unchanged registered sources, and passed analysis,
range-export, journal, bank-selection and combined integration checks. Pin the
consumer, native programs, contexts, exact all-in cache, complete checkpoint
and model identities before any scientific evaluation deal is drawn.

Consume generations 0–63, with actor weights 1–64 and their own action reach,
in A-baseline, A-relative, B-baseline, B-relative order. Authenticate generation
64 but exclude it from the played strategy. Preserve all fitted local tables
and overrides. Depths 60 and 150 remain held out of fitting.

At each of 20, 40, 60, 100, 150, 200 and 400 BB, evaluate 4,096 physical deals
with seed 9309000 + depth, in immutable batches of 32. Use all eight crossed
profiles, conditional exact preflop all-in terms, and the existing full-action
sampled-board evaluator. Error allowance is 0.05/7 per depth, at one final look.
Use the predeclared pooled and per-depth criteria and loss-tail reporting;
neither intermediate means nor test outcomes may choose a checkpoint or stop
the test early. A resource stop is permitted only before the next fresh batch.

Authenticate every archived batch when reopening a context journal and before
its final analysis. Recover only already completed outputs. A partial scientific
batch or archive without a durable publication receipt is preserved for review;
there is no automatic retry, replacement or omission of that batch.

Export the complete averaged preflop matrices and compatible-card-conditioned
arriving ranges for all four banks at all seven depths. Save all original source
evidence in the existing lossless columnar/XZ format. Retire only newly created,
owned raw duplicates after archive verification and journal commitment.

Execution limits: reserve at most 20 GB of new logical evidence against the
existing 800 GB allocated research-storage cap; keep at least 40 GB free on S:,
28 GB RAM available at admission and 24 GB while running, and use at most 2 GB
of own CUDA reservation. Share light desktop use, yield to active solves or
heavy workloads, and preserve production 56708. Twelve-hour invocations yield
at complete batch boundaries; the same admitted comparison may resume after
normal owner exit. Total evaluation time is bounded at 72 hours from admission.
Exceptions preserve a terminal failure record and do not authorize a retry.

The combined software check uses retained two-deal native fixtures, expanded
by repetition to exercise the batch transport, and synthetic routing models.
It compares the cached and reference complete-policy paths on CPU, authenticates
their actual native outputs with the journal, and checks fixed-budget orchestration
separately. Repeated fixture deals are implementation evidence only, never the
independent scientific test sample. The check does not train or use the GPU.

Successful fixed-policy evaluation still requires independent source/result
readback and plain-English analysis. The separate correctly targeted continuation
prediction diagnostic remains outstanding. These conditional heads-up values do
not establish a full-game equilibrium or accurate all-position preflop ranges.
Do not push, deploy, modify production or mark the broad research goal complete.
