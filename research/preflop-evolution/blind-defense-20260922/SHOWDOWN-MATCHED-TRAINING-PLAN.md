# Matched training with fixed showdown controls

Compare the existing postflop-integrated learner against the identical learner with the frozen BB root showdown correction. Run two fresh matched seeds (9266201 and 9266301) with baseline then corrected treatment for each seed. Card, action, reservoir, and fit random streams are identical within each pair. Policy changes may change sampled trajectories; do not describe all later observations as identical.

Each arm receives 78 updates of 512 physical deals, 39,936 deals total, with the existing 512-step CUDA fitter, 262,144-visit reservoir capacity, game, and exact initial-jam handling. Coefficients are the previously frozen all-bank averages. No coefficient tuning, bank selection, or outcome-based stopping. Retain all 78 played policies; exclude the final unplayed generation in any averaging. These are fresh training samples, not confirmatory evaluation samples.

Full restart snapshots are saved every eight updates and at update 78. The lossless evidence archive preserves every original native transport byte and every stored reservoir snapshot. Compact retirement is restricted to exact duplicates in a newly owned scratch directory, after archive readback and durable manifest publication. Existing research originals remain untouched. A process interruption can require replay of up to seven unsnapshotted updates; it cannot be represented as a completed arm.

Resource admission retains the original 800 GB allocated research ceiling, 2 GB metadata reserve, 4.25 GB new-output cap, 20 GB RAM reserve, 3 GB free GPU reserve, and 40 GB free S-drive reserve. The hard deadline is 14 hours. Archive timings and sizes from qualification are projections, not guaranteed full-run sizes; checks remain active. Stop on production activity or failed guard and retain the last complete checkpoint.

This stage produces four training arms, not a strength conclusion. Before promotion, independently read back the training updates and perform crossed payoff evaluation on a separate unseen common-deal set with unchanged native game payoffs. Examine cross-seed root-policy stability and paired payoff effects, including uncertainty; do not infer accuracy from closer appearance to Wizard or the archived variance reduction. Register evaluation counts and statistical analysis before inspecting those new payoff outcomes. No production deployment is part of this experiment.
