versus average strategies at seventeen visible decisions; this scans the
conditional hand distributions at every eligible heads-up pot-share terminal.

tree. It does not fit a model, evaluate payoffs or test a new strategy.

Use the same eight frozen 1500-iteration saves as N31: ordinary and learned
eight-player games, and ordinary/paired-Balanced/learned heads-up 40 and 100 bb

current and averaged reaches with the same f32 traversal as N31; independently
require every old count and flipped terminal to reproduce exactly. Reject

above 10% TV, and twenty largest weighted discrepancies. The diagnostic weight
is the mean of current and average products of the other seats' class masses.
It is not exact legal-card reach; weights across player/terminal pairs are not
an additive decomposition of the measured solve gap. No acceptance gate,
population inference or causal conclusion follows from this diagnostic.
