# Sequential execution of the registered strategic comparison

Attach only to the verified live process for the already-started weighted 20-to-500 segment. Record its process identity and wait for that same process. Observation timeouts do not authorize restarting, replacing or stopping it.

Once it exits, require its successful segment review, matching result hash, exact registered checkpoint schedule, successful native guard and correct restored-boundary result. Then run the seven remaining stages from `strategic-segments-v1-proposed-schedule.json`, in order, using the unchanged registered segment runner. Freeze this wrapper, its protocol, the registration, schedule and their recorded inputs before waiting. Verify them before and after every stage.

The existing segment runner remains responsible for source and executable identity, predecessor snapshot hashes, exact resumed evaluation, scientific and transfer accounting, checkpoint validation, production-idle checks, RAM/VRAM reserves, SSD capacity and its registered deadline. This wrapper never changes those limits or starts another GPU job concurrently. It never kills a user process, retries a failed stage, extends a deadline, overwrites an artifact or deletes a checkpoint. Any failure stops the chain for review.

Preserve both fixed 2,000-iteration endpoints even if a source remains underconverged. That status is recorded rather than hidden or used to extend training. After both branches finish, freeze the two final policy-result hashes and stop for the separately registered reserved-panel comparison. No reserved strategic result is read by this queue. Training completion is not evidence of improved accuracy and does not authorize a production deployment.
