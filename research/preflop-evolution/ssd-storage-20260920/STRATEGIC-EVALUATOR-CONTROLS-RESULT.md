Both 2,000-iteration training policies were frozen before these checks. The nine registered workers completed successfully: four deterministic river policies, three actual final-policy imports, and two retained development flops. No new reserved-board results were used in these controls.

The seven short river workers test policy import and accounting; their 100-iteration residuals are not convergence claims.
