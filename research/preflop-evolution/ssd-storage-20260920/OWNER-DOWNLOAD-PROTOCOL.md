Separate exact storage/performance candidate. The registered explicit traversal, projection, suit transport, player order, incoming ranges, learning schedule and floating-point math remain unchanged. Upload both players' full canonical state as before, but gather/download only the traverser's regrets and accumulated strategy, since only that player is updated. This is a byte-count prediction, not a speed claim. No pinned-buffer or upload optimization is included in this candidate.
