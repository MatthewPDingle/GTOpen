# Training and evaluation compatibility audit

Source audit performed while the first weighted 112-board seed was running, before either 112-board final policy or any new reserved-panel strategic result exists. This records an interpretation constraint; it does not alter the frozen training protocol or claim GPU parity.

The two new 112-board branches use the same `integrated_continuation_stored` executable. Both enumerate future cards explicitly and apply `SymmetricContinuationGpu`'s regret/strategy-sum projection within each public-board stabilizer and transport between suit-equivalent public branches. Their intended experimental difference remains the registered flop weights. The older 47-board reference used `integrated_continuation_paged` and plain `GpuSolver::research_continuation_sweep`, without those projection/transport steps. As already stated in `symmetric-bridge-20260919/PROJECTION-PROTOCOL.md`, tying policies changes the numerical update rule. Therefore a 112-versus-47 result cannot isolate the effect of broader flop coverage. A projected 47-board retraining would be needed for that separate causal claim.

The existing frozen-policy streamed evaluator also uses the plain, explicitly enumerated continuation update. This is a common evaluation procedure for all three source policies, not a reproduction of each source's training trajectory. It keeps every supplied preflop policy fixed, learns postflop responses, and reports both postflop residual and full preflop-plus-postflop deviation. It must be run with the same executable, numerical schedule and residual criterion for all sources. Differences involving the old 47-board source are baseline comparisons under that common procedure, not proof that flop count alone caused the change. Training-panel gaps and reserved-panel deviation gains are different quantities and must not be conflated.

The inspected examples share the original subtree, incoming class masses, combo construction, entry support cutoff of 0.00001, suit-orbit averaging at the preflop boundary, called pots/stacks (39.5/182 and 93.5/155), 4% rake capped at 6, 50% postflop bets and pot-sized raises, and at most one postflop raise per street. Fold values and the 1.75 dead-money correction are the same. Earlier folded hands are omitted throughout. New training parses through the qualified default JSON reader; the evaluator's `continuation-transfer-research` feature enables round-trip parsing to preserve imported policy bits. Reproduce the importer/continuation controls before applying that evaluator to the new sources.

Still required before the new reserved comparison: verify the retained evaluator executable hash; rerun its registered development controls and exact source-policy import checks; freeze both final 112-board policies; register the common reserved-panel run and resource limits; aggregate counterfactual values across boards before preflop maximization; validate the common physical-pair prior and independent probability/chip/rake accounting. Preserve reached and off-path behavior. A residual above 0.01 bb prevents a settled-response accuracy interpretation. No production change follows from this audit.
